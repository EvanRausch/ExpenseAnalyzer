About

ExpenseAnalyzer is a personal finance desktop application that imports bank transactions, automatically categorizes them, and provides insights into spending habits over time. It connects to financial institutions securely through Plaid to retrieve account balances and transaction data.

The application is developed and used solely by its creator and is currently in active development.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This application is a personal desktop application developed and used solely by its creator.

Information Collected

The application may access financial account information through Plaid in order to retrieve account balances and transaction data.

Use of Information

The information is used exclusively by the developer for personal financial management and testing purposes.

Sharing of Information

No personal or financial information is shared with third parties except as necessary to access financial data through Plaid and the connected financial institution.

Data Storage

Financial data is stored only on the developer's personal device unless otherwise stated.
